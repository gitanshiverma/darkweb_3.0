QUICK GUIDE

FOLLOW A CONNECTION

  1. Search an identifier.

    A full-screen search transition is followed by a popup dossier when the response arrives.

  2. Open the actor workspace.

    The account is on the left; its graph is on the right. Scroll over the graph to rotate. Scroll outside it to move down the page.

  3. Choose a card or graph branch.

    Choose a record type, then click a node or branch around the ring. Its card opens in the centre like a book. Arrow keys move between branches; the back link returns to the same account.
